# 2026-11-25 L-meta-9741 (open prove, 1442 points)

Lease: 33b011ab-f204-4e0b-b280-051ca9678f66

## Statement
There is a monotone fan-in-2 AND/OR circuit with at most 62 gates computing the edge function of a bipartite
graph on 26 vertices (left 0..12, right 13..25) with 51 edges. Best known: 63.

## Analysis
- Neighbourhoods are shifts of the perfect difference set {0,1,5,11} mod 13: the graph is the point-line
  incidence graph of PG(2,3) with the one edge (0,13) removed. So it is C4-free: two left vertices share at
  most one right neighbour, and no OR of two leaves can be shared between two stars.
- Star construction: OR over left u of x_u AND (OR of N(u)) costs edges + stars - 1 = 51 + 13 - 1 = 63.
  Fewer stars would need a vertex cover of size 12, but the graph (4-regular bipartite minus one edge) still
  has a perfect matching, so every vertex cover has 13 vertices.
- Tried absorption tricks (terms of three variables that contain an edge are harmless), e.g.
  (x_u or x_w) and (x_s or x_u A or x_w B) for a common neighbour s: correct but one gate WORSE than two stars.
- Even with a 62-gate circuit, the Lean check over 2^26 inputs would need a symbolic proof (no native_decide,
  bv_decide uses the same trust), which is real extra work.

## Outcome
No 62-gate circuit found; released with reason out_of_time (a real computer search is needed).
