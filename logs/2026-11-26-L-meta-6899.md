## Statement (decoded)
14 seed wires (inputs: points 0..6, lines 7..13 of the Fano plane) as 62-bit signatures. Columns 0..20 = the 21
incident pairs (f=1), 21..57 = false points, 58 = input e_0 alone, 59 = input e_7 alone. Columns 60/61 are "marks":
x7 carries mark 60 and x0 carries mark 61. OR ORs the marks. AND gives mark60 = A58&B60 | A60&B58 (likewise 61 with 59),
so a mark follows paths from x7 (or x0) whose AND side-inputs are on at e_0 (or e_7). Gate 0 must be an OR.
Claim: no list of <=27 gates that has the target (cols 0..20 on, marks 0) and in which every signature occurs at most
twice, a duplicate x needing a gate y = x with marks masked by cols 58/59, y != x.

## Tried
- The star circuit (OR over points of x_p AND OR(3 lines)) has 27 gates but the output carries both marks, so it is not
  a counterexample (`/tmp` simulation; logic is in tools/sigcirc_sat.py).
- SAT search (tools/sigcirc_sat.py, pysat Cadical153, naive one-hot operand-pair encoding, ~200k vars, 2.5M clauses)
  for 26 and 27 gates with the mark constraint, without the count condition. No answer in about 35 minutes.
  The sanity run with the marks left free (the star circuit is a solution) also found nothing in 10 minutes, so the encoding is far too weak.
- Even an UNSAT answer would not give a Lean proof: a 27-gate lower bound needs a certificate that the kernel can check
  without native_decide; other agents are cutting the parent into reductions (R_meta_2348_*, R_meta_4649_*).

- v2 encoding (tools/sigcirc_sat2.py, one-hot selector per operand, 8k vars / 196k clauses): the 26- and 27-gate runs
  and the 27-gate sanity run with free marks got no answer in 14 minutes (stopped at 09:20Z). Plain CDCL is not enough;
  it needs symmetry breaking (gate order, Fano automorphisms) or cubing.
