# 2026-11-28 L-meta-20579 (hard search, 300 pts, range 22 "NOT output mask 16")

Statement: some fan-in-2 AND/OR/NOT circuit with <= 170 gates represents the 32x32 matrix
(rows 0xf2bc7573, 0x59092a9d, ...) on the 1024 one-row-one-column inputs. Taken 12:02Z.

What I found
- The merged L_meta_20578.lean (same matrix, <= 180) holds an explicit 180-gate monotone circuit:
  64 inputs (0-31 rows, 32-63 cols), 148 OR gates building 32 unions, 16 ANDs, 15 ORs.
  It is 16 products (Ar u Ac) & (Br u Bc); expanded, 31 rectangles Ar x Bc, Br x Ac (one product is a
  plain rectangle). The rectangles are almost all maximal bicliques of M; no two products merge into one
  (checked all pairs, both orientations); each product covers 10-27 points no other covers.
- Cost model: 2 gates per rectangle (AND + final OR) + an OR-circuit for the operand unions. Pairing
  rectangles into products does not change the 2-per-rectangle part, but mixing a row-set and a
  column-set in one operand lets unions be shared: separate row/col OR-circuits give ~213, mixed ~180.

Tried (tools/m20579_*.py)
- Paar greedy on the whole row-function view (f = OR x_i & h_i(y), complements for dense rows): 230.
- flex.py: Paar on the 32 operands with don't-care elements (lines that add no wrong point): 180 best
  over ~2700 seeds.
- flex2.py: same with lookahead on the 30 best pairs: 180-181.
- gen2.py: operands may also use intersections of library wires (1024-bit signatures), points of M
  assigned to one owning product: 180.
- pair.py: local search over pairings of the 31 rectangles into compatible products (disjoint rows and
  cols), each scored by flex greedy: 4 processes x ~30 min, never below 180.
- emit.py: writes the Lean file from a pairing + seed in the L_meta_20578 format (checked: rebuilds a
  valid 180-gate circuit). Not used: nothing <= 170 found.

Outcome: released out_of_time. 180 looks tight for every union-sharing method; a 170 circuit would need
a different idea (NOT gates, shared non-union wires), not more greedy seeds.
